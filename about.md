I'm Sean, a partner at [ExaDev](https://exadev.io). I work as a fractional CTO
and engineering lead with startups and scaleups that need to move faster and
get to revenue.

## What I do

**Fractional CTO.** I own the technology side of the business part-time:
architecture, hiring, roadmap, budget and vendor decisions. You get senior
technical leadership at the stage where a full-time CTO doesn't yet make sense.

**Engineering lead.** I lead teams day to day, and I still write code. I set
priorities, remove blockers, review the work and make sure things ship.

**Acceleration.** When delivery has slowed down, I find out why and fix it.
The cause is usually too much scope, unclear ownership or a slow release
process.

## How I work

- Revenue first. The roadmap is ordered by what gets customers paying.
- Build for the next stage, not the one after it. Simple systems are quicker
  to ship and cheaper to change.
- Short feedback loops. Small releases, in front of users early.
- Leave the team stronger. The aim is a team that keeps its pace after I've
  stepped back.
